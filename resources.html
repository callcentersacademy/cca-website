Learning Campus

Where do you want
to start?

Three courses. Each one solves a different problem. Choose the one that speaks to where you are right now.

🎧
Module 01

English Skills

You don't need perfect English to work in a call center. You need Basic English — and with Basic English according to the CEFR you can handle every day tasks including work. This course shows you the difference between levels, teaches you to read, write, and speak for the floor, and gives you the confidence to use what you already have.

💻
Module 02

Computer Skills

A call center is a computer job. You will spend every hour sitting in front of a screen, managing multiple windows, programs, passwords, and sometimes two or three monitors with a virtual computer. Nobody warns you. Nobody teaches you. This course gives you everything you need to know before your first day.

📞
Module 03

Call Center Skills

This is the one everyone wants. The CV, the application, the interview, the floor. Six account types and everything that goes with each one. Built on the first two modules. The industry has never told you this story from beginning to end — until now.
