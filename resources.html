CCA Learning Campus

Call Centers Academy has been training call center professionals in the Dominican Republic since 2018. This campus is the free version of everything we teach — the tools, the language, the real calls, and the knowledge the industry assumes you already have but never gives you. No signup. No paywall. Free for now — use it while it lasts.

🎯
Call Center Matcher

Find the account you belong in — and the one you should avoid

Most candidates apply to whatever is open and end up in an account that is wrong for their personality. This tool asks you six questions about who you are — not what you know — and tells you which account fits you and which one would hurt you. And it tells you exactly why.

💰
Salary Calculator

Know the minimum you should accept before anyone gives you a number

Enter your real monthly expenses — rent, food, transport, phone, everything — and this tool calculates the exact hourly rate you need to actually cover your life. Use it before any interview so you never accept less than what you need.

🤖
CV Builder

Turn your work history into a CV that gets you called back

Most call center CVs get ignored because they don't say the right things. This tool is powered by artificial intelligence — you answer a few questions about your background and it builds a professional CV for you. Works even if you have never worked in a call center before.

📖
The Floor Glossary

Every word the industry expects you to already know

The BPO industry operates as if you went to school for this. Nobody explains AHT, CSAT, adherence, or nesting — not your supervisor, not your trainer, not the caller who uses industry terms and assumes you understand. This glossary gives you every word before you need it.
