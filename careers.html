Terms & Conditions
CCA Employment Agreement
1
Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
All client information, proprietary methodology, internal processes, and operational data accessed during your role with CCA are strictly confidential. You may not disclose, share, or reproduce any of this information during or after your engagement. Violation of this clause results in immediate termination and potential legal action.
2
Non-Compete Agreement
During your engagement with CCA, you may not work for, consult for, or assist any direct competitor operating within CCA's market. You are free to have other unrelated employment. This clause is limited in scope and does not restrict your general career beyond CCA's direct market.
3
Media Release & Recording Consent
All training sessions, calls, performance reviews, and interactions conducted within your role may be recorded. You authorize CCA to use these recordings for educational, quality assurance, and promotional purposes. This consent is a condition of employment and cannot be revoked once the contract is active.
4
Scope of Work & Weekly Compensation
Your role, responsibilities, compensation rate ($10–$15/hr depending on account and client), schedule, and deliverables will be defined in your individual contract. Payment is weekly in USD. Scope changes require written agreement from both parties.
5
Exit Clause & Termination
Either party may terminate the engagement with reasonable written notice as defined in your individual contract. CCA reserves the right to immediate termination for NDA violations, conduct issues, or failure to meet performance standards.
6
Technical Requirements
You are responsible for maintaining your own laptop, reliable internet connection, and power backup throughout your engagement. CCA does not provide equipment.
7
Application Process
Accepting these terms initiates your application. Our team will review your submission, conduct an interview, and follow up on next steps. A final contract will be prepared and signed before any work begins. These terms are a summary — the binding document is the individual contract.
8
Your Testimonial — Required to Apply
Before your application can be reviewed, you must submit a testimonial. Include your name, the year you attended CCA, and your answer to: "Based on your experience, why would you recommend CCA?"

Submit as a video, audio recording, or written email. Send to jobs@callcentersacademy.com with subject: [Role Code] - Testimonial - Your Full Name. Applications without this submission will not be processed.
